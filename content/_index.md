# ![alien](https://github.githubassets.com/images/icons/emoji/unicode/1f47d.png) About me

Hi, I’m Ram Krishna Katakwar, a Security Engineer III and SOC Team Lead with over 6 years of experience securing multi-cloud, containerized, and enterprise environments. My expertise lies in SIEM administration, threat detection, and incident response, with a strong track record of leading SOC integrations during mergers and acquisitions — achieving full compliance and zero incidents post-integration.

At Innovaccer, I’ve driven key security initiatives including FedRAMP, SOC2, and HITRUST readiness, developed automation pipelines that reduce response times, and built in-house CSPM and log analytics solutions saving significant operational costs. My technical toolkit spans AWS, Azure, GCP, QRadar, Wazuh, and Security Onion, along with scripting and automation using Python, Bash, Terraform, and Ansible.

I’m passionate about building resilient SOC ecosystems, mentoring incident response and forensics teams, and aligning cybersecurity strategies with business goals. My work philosophy centers on automation, measurable impact, and proactive defense

<hr/>

Cloud & SIEM

Proficient in designing and managing security operations across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
Experienced with SIEM platforms including IBM QRadar, Wazuh, and Security Onion for log ingestion, correlation, and detection engineering. Skilled in CSPM development, log pipeline creation, and custom alert automation for hybrid and containerized environments.

Network & Infrastructure Security

Extensive experience securing enterprise networks using Fortinet FortiGate, Palo Alto NGFW, and ModSecurity WAF, complemented by Cloudflare WAF integration. Adept in IDS/IPS management, SSL inspection, VPN configuration, and zero-trust architecture implementation.

Endpoint Protection & EDR

Strong background in SentinelOne and Symantec Endpoint Protection, including deployment, tuning, and response workflows. Hands-on experience with DLP solutions such as Forcepoint, CoSoSYS Endpoint Protector, and Mimecast for data governance and insider threat mitigation.

Threat Detection & Incident Response

Comprehensive understanding of SOC operations, alert triage, and threat hunting across multi-cloud environments. Experienced in building and maintaining SOC playbooks, conducting red team validation using Atomic Red Team, and optimizing detection logic to reduce false positives and improve MTTR.

Vulnerability Management & Compliance

Proficient with Nessus, OpenVAS, and StackRox (RACS) for vulnerability identification and remediation tracking.
Skilled in aligning controls with FedRAMP, SOC2, and HITRUST frameworks, as well as supporting ISO 27001 and PCI DSS audits. Proven ability to prepare evidence, documentation, and risk assessments for compliance readiness.

Digital Forensics & Investigations

Hands-on experience with forensic tools including FTK Imager, EnCase, Autopsy, Cellebrite UFED, and Volatility for evidence acquisition and analysis. Knowledgeable in chain-of-custody management, memory forensics, and mobile data recovery for incident and fraud investigations.

Offensive Security & Red Teaming

Practical experience using Nmap, Metasploit, Cobalt Strike, Burp Suite, Caldera, and Gophish for controlled red team simulations, phishing campaigns, and web application security testing. Strong understanding of threat emulation, adversary techniques (MITRE ATT&CK), and detection validation.

Programming, Automation & Infrastructure as Code
